# Life OS — User Guide

A calm, local-first personal operating system. Everything you enter is stored on
your device only. The loop is simple: **Plan → Track → Review → Learn → Adjust.**

---

## 1. What Life OS is

A single place to plan your day, track what you actually do (habits, fitness,
diet, sleep, reading, learning), reflect each night, and review each week and
month. It turns your own history into honest, transparent insight — no accounts,
no cloud, no AI guessing. Every number can be explained.

## 2. The sidebar

- **PLAN** — Today, Goals, Tasks, Projects
- **REVIEW** — Daily review, Weekly review, Monthly review
- **TRACK** — Fitness, Diet, Sleep, Routines, Reading
- **SYSTEM** — Settings & data

## 3. Every morning

Open **Today**. It answers "what should I do today?" — not statistics.

1. Check **Priorities** (high/urgent tasks) and **Today's tasks**.
2. Tick off **Habits** scheduled for today.
3. Run your **Routines** (Morning / Day / Night / HGH).
4. Glance at **Goals** and any **Attention needed**.
5. Add tasks as they come up (the quick-add bar at the top; `!` = high, `!!` = urgent).

## 4. During the day

Log things where they happen, when they happen:

- **Tasks** — add, complete, set priority/due date, link to a goal or project.
- **Fitness** — start a workout, add exercises and sets (bodyweight or weighted),
  finish it. Log your **bodyweight** whenever you weigh in.
- **Diet** — log meals (kcal + macros) and water.
- **Sleep** — log duration, or bedtime + wake time (it handles crossing midnight).
- **Reading** — update progress, add notes, mark a book finished.
- **Learning** — complete your Learning habit/routine step (see §12).

## 5. Every night — Daily Review

Open **Daily review**. A "Today at a glance" strip shows the day's facts (habits,
tasks, workout, calories/protein/water, sleep, weight, reading) so you don't have
to remember everything. Then you write:

- **What went well**
- **What didn't** (mistakes/problems)
- **What I learned**
- **Top priority tomorrow**
- **Day rating** (1–5)

Your words are yours — the app never fills them in. Saving shows a read-only
"Today's review" card; you can edit it any time that day.

## 6. What Weekly Review tells you

Open **Weekly review** (uses the actual 7 days of the selected week; use Prev /
This week to navigate). Top-to-bottom, most important first:

1. **Attention needed** — a few data-backed, conservative alerts.
2. **Weekly scorecard** — a transparent score per area (each shows its formula).
3. **This week vs last week** — habit %, tasks, avg rating.
4. **Habit consistency** — completion rate + the exact days you missed.
5. **Daily ratings**, **Tasks**, **Fitness**, **Sleep**, **Diet**, **Reading**.
6. **What went well** / **Common problems** (recurring themes from your reviews).
7. **What I learned** — everything you wrote that week, by day.

## 7. What Monthly Review tells you

Open **Monthly review** (fully month-scoped — August uses 31 days, September 30,
February 28/29). Same shape as the week but over the month, plus **This month vs
last month** and a **Bodyweight** summary for the month. Read it to see what
actually moved and what slipped.

## 8. How Goals work

Goals nest: **Vision → Year → Quarter → Month → Week → Milestones**. Child goals
are indented under their parent. For a measurable goal, the **Intelligence** panel
shows current, target, remaining, recent pace, required pace, status
(ahead / on track / behind), and a projected date — **only when there's enough
history**. Otherwise it says "Not enough data yet" rather than guessing.

- **Bodyweight goals** (e.g. 44 → 56 kg) read their current value from your
  **Fitness bodyweight history** — one source of truth. Log weight on Fitness;
  the goal updates itself.
- Set a goal's status with the one-click pills: Active / Completed / On hold / Archived.

## 9. Projects vs Goals

- A **Goal** is an outcome you're moving toward (measurable or milestone-based).
- A **Project** is a body of work with a status (e.g. "Prop firm challenge") that
  groups **tasks**. Link a task to a project on the Tasks page.

Use Goals for *where you're going*, Projects for *the work you're doing*. They
don't duplicate each other — a task can optionally point at one of each.

## 10. Tasks vs Habits

- **Task** — a one-off thing to finish ("Call the bank"). It has a due date and
  priority; you complete it once.
- **Habit** — something recurring you want to keep doing ("Brush teeth", "Learning").
  It has a schedule; you tick it each day it's due, building a streak/history.

## 11. How Routines work

A **Routine** (Morning / Day / Night / HGH) is an ordered group of **habits** you
run together. Completing a routine step ticks its underlying habit — there is no
double-counting. Routine progress is derived from those habit completions.

## 12. How Learning works

Learning is intentionally just a **habit/routine** — not a separate content system.
Whenever you learn something (a book, article, course, coding, a project), tick
your **Learning** habit and jot the takeaway in that night's **What I learned**.
Weekly/Monthly Review then show your Learning consistency, missed days, and a
by-day record of what you learned.

## 13. How the HGH routine fits in

**HGH** (stretching, hanging, jumping, sprints) is a **routine of habits**, kept
separate from strength training on purpose. Completing HGH steps **never** creates
workout volume or counts as a muscle-gaining workout — it won't distort your
Fitness numbers or scorecard. It's tracked purely as habit consistency.

## 14. Fitness & bodyweight

- **Workouts**: start → add exercises → add sets (reps, and weight only if it's a
  weighted exercise) → **Finish workout**. Finished workouts become a clean,
  read-only history record.
- **Bodyweight**: log a reading whenever you weigh in. The **Bodyweight trend**
  card shows latest/starting/change and, with 2+ readings on different days, a
  trend line and per-week change. One reading shows the value without inventing a
  trend. This history is the single source of truth for bodyweight goals.

## 15. Diet, Sleep, Reading

- **Diet**: log meals and water against your saved **minimums** (Settings). These
  are minimums — eating *more* than the minimum is a **success**, never a failure.
- **Sleep**: log duration (or bed/wake times). Review shows your average and how
  many nights met your target.
- **Reading**: track current/finished books, progress, and notes. With enough
  finished books, Reading shows a pace and projection toward your book goal.

## 16. Back up your data (export)

Settings & data → **Export JSON**. This downloads a complete backup of everything
(tasks, habits, completions, routines, goals, milestones, projects, reviews,
fitness, bodyweight, diet, sleep, reading, and settings). Keep it somewhere safe.

## 17. Restore a backup (import)

Settings & data → paste the backup JSON → **Import & replace**. This replaces the
current data with the backup. If the JSON is invalid it's rejected safely — your
current data is not touched. To wipe and start fresh, use **Clear all data**
(two-step confirmation).

## 18. What the scorecards mean

Each area (Habits, Tasks, Fitness, Diet, Sleep, Learning, Daily review) gets a
percentage **with its formula shown** — e.g. "Sleep 80% = 4/5 nights met target".
The overall is the average of the areas that have data. Nothing is a mystery
number; if you can't explain it, it isn't shown.

## 19. What "No data" (—) means

If you've never tracked an area, it shows **"—" / "No data"** and is **excluded**
from the overall score. Not tracking something is never counted as a failure or a
0%. The score only reflects what you actually logged.

## 20. What "Attention needed" means

A short, conservative list of genuinely actionable things, each explaining *why*
with the numbers behind it (e.g. "Protein minimum missed 4 of 7 logged days",
"No workout logged in 8 days"). A single off day won't trigger a warning, and
things that are going well are never framed as problems.

---

**In one line:** every morning, act from **Today**; every night, reflect in
**Daily review**; every week and month, learn from **Review**; adjust; repeat.
